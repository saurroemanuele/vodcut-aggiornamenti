FAQ

Is NoonFrame really free?

Yes. Video, graphics and every feature that runs on your computer, with no subscription and no watermark. You only need a free account: sign in with Google in one click.

What about AI?

Background removal and subtitles are free and run on your computer. Features that use external AI services (images, generative fill, stories) use your own AI keys: you only pay for what you use, directly to the service.

Can I open Photoshop files?

Yes: open PSDs with their layers and save them back as PSD. You can also export PNG, JPG, WebP, PDF (CMYK for print too), SVG, GIF and MP4.

Why do I need an account?

So we can send you updates, listen to your requests and keep NoonFrame free and safe from abuse. We see which tools you use, never your videos.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your computer.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. With an NVIDIA card, subtitles are faster.

Does it work without streams?

Yes: any video, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5. And in the Graphics section you make thumbnails, posts and graphics from scratch.

Which languages?

English and Italian. Subtitles recognize the language you speak.
