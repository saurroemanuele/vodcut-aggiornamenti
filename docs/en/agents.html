Connect your AI agent to NoonFrame.

Ask Claude or your agent to turn your streams and videos into vertical clips while you do something else.

Two steps

1Add NoonFrame

Claude (web and app)Open Customize › Connectors, tap Add custom connector, paste the address and tap Add. Then tap Connect next to NoonFrame.
Claude Codeclaude mcp add --transport http noonframe https://jhoidpugjjvvkjccyrxg.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp then /mcp to sign in.
Codexcodex mcp add noonframe --url https://jhoidpugjjvvkjccyrxg.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp then codex mcp login noonframe.
ChatGPT, Cursor and othersAdd a remote MCP server with this address.

2Sign in and tap Connect

A NoonFrame page opens. Sign in with the same Google account you use in the app and tap Connect. Your agent is ready.

What you can ask

  • Make clips from yesterday's stream, twitch.tv/videos/…
  • How many credits do I have left?
  • In clip 2 put the subtitles in the middle and remove the title.
  • Redo the clips of this video, only the funniest moments.

It can

  • See your plan and credits
  • Read a Twitch, Kick or YouTube link and tell you the cost
  • Make the clips, follow them and show you titles and videos
  • Edit a clip: layout, title, subtitles, start and end

It can't

  • See the files on your computer
  • Buy anything or change your plan
  • Change your account or see anyone else's

You decide

  • Your agent always tells you what a video costs before making clips.
  • Clips cost the same credits as web clips.
  • Disconnect an agent anytime from noonframe.com/collega.html.

Questions

Do I need a subscription?

Yes. AI agents are for Starter, Pro and Ultimate subscribers. Pick a plan.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Connect it once on the Claude website and use it from the Claude app on your phone too.

It says authentication is needed?

Reconnect NoonFrame from your agent (in Claude Code with /mcp) and sign in again.

What about clips in the desktop app?

For now your agent uses web clips. The NoonFrame desktop app stays free and works as always.
